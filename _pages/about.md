### Research Area & Interests
* **Research Area:** Digital Cinema, Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Moving-Image Heritage — with a focus on Film Preservation, Restoration, Emerging Technologies, and Indian Cinema.
* **Core:** Digital Cinema, AI, Moving-Image Heritage
* **Preservation:** Archives, Restoration, Digital Cultural Memory
* **Cinema:** Indian Cinema, Global South Cinema, Eco-Cinema
* **Distinctive:** Indigenous Audiovisual Heritage
* **Technology:** VFX, Virtual Production, Computational Film Studies
